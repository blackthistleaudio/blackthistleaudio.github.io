Who We Are

Built on craft,
driven by detail

Black Thistle Audio was founded on a simple belief: great sound is never an accident. It's the result of experienced engineers, quality equipment, meticulous preparation, and a genuine commitment to getting it right.

Based in Edinburgh, we've worked across the full spectrum of live events — from intimate fringe venues and corporate boardrooms to major outdoor stages. We know Scotland's venues, we understand the challenges of its weather and acoustics, and we bring the same level of care whether the audience is 50 or 5,000.

Our engineers are hands-on at every stage — from initial consultation through to the final soundcheck. This is our craft!
